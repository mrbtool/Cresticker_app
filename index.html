Name Sticker: make name labels in minutes

Design clean, colourful name stickers and labels on your phone, put them on a printable A4 sheet, and print them or save them as a PDF. No design skills needed.

✍️

Text with your own fonts

Choose from the font list or import any .ttf / .otf font. Change size, colour and outline.

🖼️

Stickers and shapes

Add stickers from the app or your gallery, plus lines, circles, stars, hearts, arrows and more. Resize them by dragging the corner dots.

🎨

Colours, gradients and borders

Solid colours, ready-made gradients with an angle slider, background images, and borders with their own corner radius.

📐

Smart alignment

Guide lines and a small vibration help you line up texts, stickers and shapes perfectly.

📄

Papers (A4 sheets)

Save each sticker onto a named paper. Every paper holds 8 stickers and shows up as a page you can swipe.

🖨️

Print or save as PDF

Tap a page to select it (blue tick), then print or save only the pages you chose.

How it works

  1. Design. Add a name, pick a font, then add stickers, shapes, colours or a background.
  2. Save. Tap Save and choose a paper name. Use Draft to keep unfinished work.
  3. Arrange. Open My Stickers and swipe through your papers. Delete a sticker or a whole paper any time.
  4. Print. Tap the pages you want, then choose Print or Save PDF.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about creating, saving and printing your stickers.

How do I make my first name sticker?
Open the Content tab and tap Text, type a name and press Add to canvas. Drag it where you want it. Use the Text tab to change size, colour, stroke and font.
Why can’t I save my sticker?
Saving needs an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data), because the app is supported by ads. Saving is also blocked while Private DNS is set to a provider hostname. Turn Private DNS off in your phone’s network settings, then try again.
What is the difference between Save and Draft?
Save puts the finished sticker in My Stickers. Draft puts it in Drafts, a separate place for work in progress. Both ask which paper to save on.
What is a paper?
A paper is one named A4 sheet. When you save, you type a paper name (for example “Class 5 – Section A”). Stickers with the same paper name go on the same sheet, up to 8 per sheet. Each paper appears as a page you can swipe in My Stickers.
How do I print or save selected pages as a PDF?
Open My Stickers and tap a page. A blue tick shows it is selected. Tap more pages if you like, then choose Print or Save PDF. If no page is selected, all pages are used.
How do I delete a sticker or a paper?
Tap the red ✕ on a sticker (or long-press it) to delete just that sticker. Tap the red Delete chip at the top-left of a page to delete the whole paper.
Can I use my own fonts?
Yes. Open Text → Font and tap Import Font, then choose a .ttf or .otf file. The font is added to the list and stays available next time. Only import fonts you have the right to use.
Can I use my own pictures as stickers or backgrounds?
Yes. Tap Sticker or Image and choose Choose from device, or pick one of the images that come with the app. Use Img Size to resize a background and long-press the label to move it.
How do I resize shapes and stickers?
Tap the item to select it, then drag one of the corner dots. Shapes can be stretched freely, and stickers keep their proportions. You can also pinch with two fingers, or use the Size slider for shapes.
How do I line things up perfectly?
Drag an item near the centre or near the edges of other items. Pink guide lines appear, the item snaps into place and the phone vibrates briefly. You can also drag on empty space on the label to move the selected item without covering it with your finger.
How do I change the order of items (layers)?
Select an item, then use Forward or Backward in the Content tab to move it up or down one layer.
Where are my stickers stored?
Only on your phone, inside the app’s private storage. We do not upload them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted, so save your PDFs if you want a copy.
Where is my PDF saved?
Save PDF stores the file in the app’s Documents folder. To keep a copy somewhere else, use Print and choose “Save as PDF” in your phone’s print dialog.
The keyboard hides my text box. What should I do?
The typing area moves above the keyboard automatically. If it does not, close the keyboard and tap the text box again, and make sure your phone’s keyboard is up to date.
How do I contact support?
Open the Feedback tab, or email support@example.com.
